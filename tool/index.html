Build vs. Buy

The decision most agents get wrong. Here is the rule.

🔨 Build when

  • The tool doesn't exist yet.
  • The tool exists but can't see your data.
  • You're solving a problem specific to your business.
  • You have lots of time and not lots of money.

🛒 Buy when

  • You're busy. Your time is worth more than the tool costs.
  • The tool exists and matches your jam.
  • You're solving a problem thousands of agents share.
  • Setup would take longer than a few weeks.

Most of you, most of the time: buy. Don't waste a Saturday building what someone has already built for you.
